Video Manager

Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 10, 2026

Video Manager is a personal automation tool operated by its owner (contact: pinakigraham@gmail.com). It uses Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API to manage and upload videos on the owner's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the app may access: your basic Google account information (name, email address), and YouTube channel data needed for the requested actions, such as uploading videos, setting titles, descriptions, thumbnails and privacy status, and reading upload status.

2. How the data is used

Data is used only to perform the video management actions you initiate. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the app's function.

3. Storage and security

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored securely on the owner's own system solely to keep the automation running. No YouTube or Google account data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties, except as required to call Google APIs or by law.

4. YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Video Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of any stored tokens or data, email pinakigraham@gmail.com; requests are handled within 30 days.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated occasionally. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
